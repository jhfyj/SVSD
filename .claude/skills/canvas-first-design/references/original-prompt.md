# Original prompt (as saved, 2026-10-14)

Written for the Lunagraph canvas. The adapted version is in [../SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).

```
use the lunagraph canvas as your thinking tool. don't write any code until the design is decided.

1. describe the job to be done. who the user is, what they're trying to do, and what "done" looks like for them.

2. describe the solution and the requirements. what has to be true for it to work. what's out of scope.

3. design on the canvas. bring the screen and the components in. try a few directions and variations. no functionality yet.

4. rate each one. does it serve the job to be done? is the ui clear? is the ux right? be honest. iterate until your rating is 10/10.

5. prototype the winner. make it work on the canvas. then pretend to be the user and try to do the job. if it's not good, go back to 4. iterate, iterate, iterate.

6. refine every state. loading, empty, error, mobile.

7. implement it in the codebase (if your human asks for it). bring it back to the canvas and check it against the design.

8. hand off. help your human understand what to look at: what you did, what you considered, what you decided and why, and what still needs review. annotate it on the canvas. don't make them read the log. keep your process documented, but make it clear what to look at first.
```
